TITLE
ATR Position Size & Stop Planner

DESCRIPTION (paste as-is. TradingView forbids links, websites, social handles, contact details and company names here. Keep it that way.)

Most traders pick a size first and squeeze the stop to fit it. This planner works the other way round. Risk first, stop second, size last:

1. Set your risk budget for the trade.
2. Let volatility set the stop. The stop sits a multiple of ATR from your planned entry (default 1.5 x ATR 14), rounded UP to a whole tick so it is never tighter than planned.
3. Let the stop set the size. Maximum contracts = risk budget / (stop in points x value per point + costs per contract), rounded DOWN, so the estimated stop risk never exceeds the budget.

THE DASHBOARD
- The maximum position size is the headline number, with the symbol, timeframe and risk mode above it.
- Risk budget, estimated stop risk, budget remaining and budget used (%).
- ATR, volatility versus its normal level, the stop in points and ticks, and the cost per contract.
- Adjustable text size, compact or full layout, and four panel positions.

STANDARD VERSUS MICRO
On ES/MES, NQ/MNQ, YM/MYM, RTY/M2K, CL/MCL, GC/MGC, SI/SIL, 6E/M6E and BTC/MBT, an optional block compares both contracts for the same stop distance. Each contract uses its own tick size and value per point, and its own round-turn cost. You see the maximum contracts, estimated stop risk and remaining budget for each side by side. It works from either chart, the standard or the micro.

RISK MODES
- Fixed: an amount per trade.
- Percent of account: account size x risk %.
- Prop firm: the remaining drawdown allowance, less a safety buffer, divided by the losing streak you want to survive. The panel shows the remaining allowance against the original, and how much is used.
- Optional remaining daily loss allowance: caps risk per trade at half of what is left today, and shows how many full stops still fit.

Prop firms define drawdown differently. A trailing limit moves up with your peak balance; a static one does not. Enter the current remaining allowance from your firm's dashboard. This is a planning aid. It does not check your account against any firm's rules; staying within them remains your responsibility.

PLANNING LEVELS ON THE CHART
Entry, stop and an optional target (as an R multiple) are drawn as planning levels, with labels prefixed PLAN. Choose Clean (lines only), Prices, or Prices + risk (estimated stop risk at the stop, and the hypothetical reward at the target). Planning for Long or Short shades the risk and target zones. These levels are drawings only: no orders are placed and nothing is executed. A target reward is hypothetical. It is only realised if price reaches the target and the fill matches the plan.

OTHER SETTINGS
- ATR length, stop multiple, and ATR timeframe (the same or a higher timeframe than the chart; the panel warns if it is lower).
- "Use the last closed bar's ATR" keeps the numbers fixed while the current bar forms. It reads the previous completed bar, so it does not look ahead.
- Value per point: leave at 0 to use the chart symbol's value, or type one in.
- Planned entry: 0 uses the last price.

VOLATILITY VERSUS NORMAL
ATR is compared with its own average over the last 100 bars (adjustable): above 1.25x is elevated, below 0.8x is quiet. When volatility expands, the stop widens and the size shrinks, so the money at risk stays inside the budget.

ALERTS
- Position size changed.
- Volatility expanded (ATR crossed above 1.25x its normal).

LIMITATIONS
- A planning aid, not a signal. It does not tell you when, where or which way to trade.
- With "closed bar" off, ATR and the size can change until the current bar closes.
- Contract specifications come from the chart symbol and a built-in table of the pairs above. Check them against your broker's.
- Amounts are in the chart symbol's currency; the panel notes when that is not USD.
- Slippage, gaps and fast markets can fill a stop beyond its level. Costs are included only if you enter them.
- Very small budgets can round down to 0 contracts.
