TITLE
ATR Position Size & Stop Planner

DESCRIPTION (paste as-is. TradingView forbids links, websites, social handles, contact details and company names here. Keep it that way.)

Most traders pick a size first and squeeze the stop to fit it. This planner works the other way round:

1. Choose your risk for the trade.
2. Let volatility set the stop. The stop sits a multiple of ATR from your entry (default 1.5 × ATR 14), rounded up to a whole tick so it is never tighter than planned.
3. Let the stop set the size. Contracts = risk ÷ (stop in points × dollars per point + costs per contract), always rounded DOWN, so the actual risk never exceeds your risk budget.

WHAT YOU SEE
• A dashboard with your risk budget, ATR, volatility versus its normal level, the stop in points and ticks, dollars per point, risk per contract, the number of contracts and the actual risk.
• The micro-contract alternative on ES, NQ, YM, RTY, CL and GC (MES, MNQ, MYM, M2K, MCL, MGC at one-tenth the point value). When one full contract already risks too much, the panel shows 0 and the micro line shows what still fits.
• Optional entry, stop and target levels on the chart, with the stop distance in ticks and the target as an R multiple.

RISK MODES
• Fixed $: a dollar amount per trade.
• % of account: account size × risk %.
• Prop firm: maximum drawdown ÷ the losing streak you want to survive (default 10).
• Optional daily loss limit: caps risk per trade at half the limit and shows how many full losses fit in the day.

OTHER SETTINGS
• ATR length, stop multiple, and ATR timeframe (the same or a higher timeframe than the chart, for example a 5-minute ATR on a 1-minute chart).
• "Use the last closed bar's ATR" keeps the numbers fixed while the current bar forms. Off, they update live.
• Dollars per point: leave at 0 to use the chart symbol's value, or type one in (ES 50, MES 5, NQ 20, MNQ 2, CL 1000, GC 100).
• Round-turn costs per contract: added to the risk per contract.
• Planned entry: 0 uses the last price. Set a price to plan a specific level.
• Compact or full panel, and panel position.

VOLATILITY VERSUS NORMAL
ATR is compared with its own average over the last 100 bars (adjustable). Above 1.25× is shown as elevated, below 0.8× as quiet. When volatility expands, the stop widens and the size shrinks, so the dollars at risk stay the same.

ALERTS
• Position size changed.
• Volatility expanded (ATR crossed above 1.25× its normal).

LIMITATIONS
• This is a planning aid, not a signal. It does not tell you when, where or which way to trade.
• With "closed bar" off, ATR and the size can change until the current bar closes.
• Point values come from the chart symbol. Check them against your broker's contract specifications, especially on non-futures symbols.
• Slippage, gaps and fast markets can fill a stop beyond its level. Costs are only included if you enter them.
• Very small risk budgets can round down to 0 contracts.

Educational tool. Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss.
