Common questions
My NinjaTrader email is different from my Whop email.

That's fine. Sign in with Whop as usual, then enter whichever email your NinjaTrader account uses. The licenses go to the NinjaTrader email.

I don't have a NinjaTrader account yet.

You can create one for free. Then come back here and use that email in step 2.

Do you need my NinjaTrader password?

Never. We only need the email address on the account, confirmed with a one-time code.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, the indicators deactivate automatically. Resubscribe any time and they come back.
