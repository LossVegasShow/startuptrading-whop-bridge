COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Do I need a NinjaTrader account?

Yes, for indicator access. Existing NinjaTrader customers verify the email associated with their account. New customers can create an account and return to activation. You will never be asked for your NinjaTrader password by StartUpTrading.

Is the subscription managed here?

No. Whop remains the subscription and payment platform. StartUpTrading checks active entitlements and manages access to the three listed indicators.

When do I receive my indicators?

After your eligible Whop membership and NinjaTrader email are verified, the licensing service processes activation. The member page shows pending, active, or unsuccessful activation clearly. Confirmation instructions are sent only after confirmed provisioning.

How do I cancel?

Manage your recurring membership through Whop under the terms accepted at checkout. Indicator access follows the actual paid entitlement period and may expire when eligible membership access ends.

Does membership guarantee profitable trading?

No. Markets involve financial risk and no indicator, trader, or educational service can guarantee results.
