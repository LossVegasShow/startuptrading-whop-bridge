What do I need to use the tools?

NinjaTrader 8 on a Windows computer and a NinjaTrader account. The account is free to create if you don't have one yet.

I already have NinjaTrader. Does that work?

Yes. After you join, you enter the email on your existing NinjaTrader account and the tools are licensed to it. It can be different from the email you use on Whop.

How fast do I get access?

Discord and masterclass access are granted at checkout. Your tools are licensed as soon as you confirm your NinjaTrader email on the activation page, which takes about three minutes.

Do new tools cost extra?

No. When Jack releases a new tool, it's added to the membership and licensed to every active member's NinjaTrader account through the same activation platform. No separate purchase.

Do you need my NinjaTrader password?

Never. We only need the email address on your account, confirmed with a one-time code.

Is there an annual plan?

Yes: ${{price_annual}} a year, which works out to ${{price_annual_per_month}} a month (about {{annual_savings_pct}}% less than paying monthly). Same tools, same Discord and masterclass access. Both plans renew automatically until you cancel. Pick "Annual" in the pricing section and check out right there.

I already bought ATR Order Automation or the Trend Scanner. Can I join?

Yes. You keep the license you bought; the membership never changes or removes it. When you join, the tools you don't own yet are licensed to the same NinjaTrader account, and you get the Discord, the masterclasses and everything else.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep everything until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, the tools deactivate and Discord and masterclass access end. You can rejoin anytime and they come back.

Can I try something before I pay?

Yes. Jack's {{free_indicator_name}} is free. Get it here, use it on your charts, and join when you want the full toolkit and the trade room.

Are these trade signals or financial advice?

No. They're analysis and order-management tools, and the room and masterclasses are for education and discussion. They support your trading process; you make your own trading decisions, and your results depend on them and on the market.
