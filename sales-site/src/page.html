01 · The indicators

Built for the decisions you make in the seconds before you click.

Each one does a single job well. Together they answer the three questions every trade starts with: which way, where, and how much risk.

#2071 · Trend Scanner and Momentum Selector

Know which way the market's leaning before you take a side.

TSMS reads trend direction and momentum together and shows you when they agree, so you stop fighting the tape on low-conviction setups.

  • Trend and momentum in one view
  • Clear agreement / disagreement states
  • Works on the timeframes you already trade

#2205 · TSMS & Fibs

Trend context and pullback levels on the same chart.

Everything TSMS does, with Fibonacci levels drawn for you. Pullback zones sit right next to the trend read, so you're not redrawing retracements every swing.

  • Fib levels plotted automatically
  • Pullback zones in trend context
  • Less manual drawing, cleaner charts

#2067 · ATR Order Automation

Stops and targets sized to how much the market is actually moving.

Instead of a fixed tick stop that's too tight on fast days and too loose on slow ones, ATR Order Automation sets your order distances from the Average True Range, consistently, every time.

  • Volatility-based stop and target distances
  • Consistent risk from trade to trade
  • Fewer manual order adjustments
