#2067 · ATR Order Automation

Risk set. Orders placed.

ATR Order Automation sets your position size from your dollar risk and the market's current ATR, then places your stop and target in NinjaTrader as you enter. Two contracts on a quiet day and two on a fast day stop being the same mistake.

  • Contracts set from your dollar risk
  • Stop and target placed automatically
  • Same dollar risk in fast and slow markets

Try the method

Same risk. Fast market or slow.

Set your risk and drag ATR. The stop sits 1.5 × ATR away and the contracts adjust, so a losing trade costs the same in a quiet market and a fast one. ATR Order Automation does this math on every entry.

03 · Who's behind it

Built by traders, for traders.

Jack Gleason has traded since 2008. He hosted his own show on tastytrade from 2012, later traded at a proprietary firm, and holds a Series 56 license. Today he trades futures live on NinjaTrader Live, NinjaTrader's YouTube show, several times a week.

Everything he builds starts with one rule: set the stop from ATR, then size the position so a fast market and a slow market risk the same dollars when the trade is wrong. The StartUpTrading tools are the ones he built to do that in his own account, and members get each new one as he releases it.

Yes. After you join, you enter the email on your existing NinjaTrader account and the tools are licensed to it. It can be different from the email you use on Whop.

How fast do I get access?

Discord and masterclass access are granted at checkout. Your tools are licensed as soon as you confirm your NinjaTrader email on the activation page, which takes about three minutes.

No. When Jack releases a new tool, it's added to the membership and licensed to every active member's NinjaTrader account through the same activation platform. No separate purchase.
