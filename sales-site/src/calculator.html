How it works

Size from risk, not from conviction.

Contracts = risk per trade ÷ (stop in points × point value), rounded down. Rounding down means the real risk is never more than you set.

ATR stops move with the market. When volatility expands, the stop gets wider and the size gets smaller, so a quiet day and a news day risk the same dollars.

Prop-firm planner: risk per trade = max drawdown ÷ the losing streak you want to survive. The suggested daily stop is three losing trades. If your firm has a daily loss limit, the daily stop never exceeds it, and risk per trade is capped at half of it.
