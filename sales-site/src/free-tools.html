The idea behind them

Size every trade from risk.

Every tool here starts from the same rule: decide what you're willing to lose first, let volatility set the stop, and let the stop set the size. Contracts are always rounded down, so the real risk never exceeds the risk you chose.

ATR (average true range) measures how far a market typically moves per bar. Placing the stop a multiple of ATR away keeps it outside normal noise. When volatility expands the stop widens and the size shrinks, so a quiet session and a news-driven session risk the same dollars.

The tools are planning aids. They don't tell you when or which way to trade, and they can't remove the risk of trading futures.
