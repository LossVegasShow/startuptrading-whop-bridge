Troubleshooting

Something not right?

Still stuck? Email {{support_email}} with the email on your NinjaTrader account.

A tool says it isn't licensed

Restart NinjaTrader once. Then check you're signed in to NinjaTrader with the same email you confirmed during activation, and that your activation status shows your tools as active.

I can't find a tool after importing

Restart NinjaTrader, then look in the StartupTrading folder: ATR Order Automation is under the chart's Strategies list and the Trend Scanner under Indicators. If it still isn't there, import the .zip again and contact support.

The import shows an error

Make sure you're importing the .zip itself (not an unzipped folder) and that NinjaTrader 8 is up to date. Then try again, or contact support with a screenshot of the message.

Can I use the tools on another computer?

Yes. Install NinjaTrader 8, sign in with the same NinjaTrader account and repeat steps 2–4. The licenses follow your account.

I want to use a different NinjaTrader account

Contact support first. We move your licenses safely, so don't buy a second membership.

What about new tools?

When Jack releases a new tool, it's licensed to your account automatically. You'll get the download and you import it the same way.
