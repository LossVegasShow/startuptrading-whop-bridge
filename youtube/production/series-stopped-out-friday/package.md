# Stopped-Out Friday (weekly template)

**When:** Weekly, every Friday from 4 Dec 2026. Results in ticks only, every losing trade, at least one good loss.

**Structure:** The Breakdown, once per losing trade · **Length:** 6–10 minutes · **Chapters:** one per trade, plus "The week in one line"

*[Cold open: montage. Each of the week's Risk Cards flips to its result, two seconds each, losses in ticks.]*

> Every trading channel shows you their best trades. This is every losing trade I took this week. [N] of them. I'll show you the plan for each one, what happened, and whether I'd take it again. The worst one's last.

*[Cut to Jack.]*

For each trade, Jack covers four beats in about 60–90 seconds:

1. **The plan:** "[Day], [market], [time]. Here's the Risk Card before I clicked: entry zone [x], stop [n] ticks, [n] contracts." *[The card on screen.]*
2. **What happened:** "[One or two sentences, on the chart.]"
3. **The result:** "Stopped out, [n] ticks, exactly what the card said." *[The card flips.]*
4. **The verdict:** a stamp on screen, either **GOOD LOSS** (followed the plan, would take it again) or **SLOPPY LOSS** (broke a rule), with one sentence on why.

> *[After the last trade:]* So that's the week. [N] losses, [n] of them good losses I'd take again tomorrow, [n] I wouldn't. The difference wasn't the market. It was [the one lesson from the week].
>
> Which one would you have skipped? Tell me in the comments, I read them all. This is every Friday.

**Rules for the series:** results only in ticks, never dollars; include every losing trade, not a selection, or the format loses its point; always at least one "good loss" so the lesson is that losing on plan is normal. **Why it holds:** the countdown plus "the worst one's last" is an open loop to the end; the verdict stamp gives a small payoff every 90 seconds; it's the most honest format in the niche, which is why it gets shared.
