## Positioning

Trade with the tools Jack uses live, and watch him use them. Competitors sell
software; StartUpTrading is the tools, the method and the trader together.

Message pillars:
1. process: process over predictions. ATR sizing, defined risk, a trend read
   before every trade.
2. live: see it live. Show the tools on real charts, in real time.
3. bundle: everything in one place. Three tools, the room and Jack for the
   price of a NinjaTrader monthly license.
4. easy_start: licensed in about 3 minutes, cancel anytime, free indicator first.

Signature line: "I show you my risk, not my profits." It turns the compliance
rules into the brand: no lifestyle shots, nothing but the process.

## Voice

Plain, direct, a working trader talking to another trader. Short sentences.
Specific (name the market, the time, the setup). No hype, no emojis in emails,
at most one emoji in a short-form post. First person when writing as Jack.

## Hard rules (compliance)

- Never claim or imply profits, income, win rates, or that anyone will make
  money. No P&L, account balances or "I made $X".
- Never use superlatives that can't be proven ("best in the world", "only
  tool like it", "offered nowhere else").
- No backtests or hypothetical results.
- No urgency tricks (fake countdowns, "only N spots left").
- Testimonials may describe the tools, support or the room, never money made.
- Keep the room educational: "here's my read", never "buy now at X".
- Every post, caption and email ends with the risk line:
  "Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for
  every investor. Education only, not financial advice."
